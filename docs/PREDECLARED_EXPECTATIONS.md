Copied from the proposal (dated 18 Oct). Do not edit after running experiments.
1. Baseline sensitivity will be lower in the darker group than in the lighter group.
2. Reweighting will narrow the gap but not remove it.
3. A gap is real only if its 95% interval stays away from zero; if the darker group has too few melanomas, say underpowered and do not read the sign.
4. If manual review shows most "darker" ISIC images are dark photographs rather than dark skin, that is a finding; restrict the analysis to reviewed images.
